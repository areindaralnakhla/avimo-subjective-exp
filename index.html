Subjective Evaluation (~3-5 minutes)

This study evaluates the quality of a 3D avatar reconstructed from a dance video.

First, you will view the original dancer's video; then you will evaluate the reconstructed avatar in an interactive A-pose viewer; and finally, you'll evaluate the reconstructed motion video. Please rate each item on the clearly labeled 5-point rating scale.
Estimated participation time: ~3–5 minutes.

Consent

Your participation is voluntary and anonymous. Responses will be used for research purposes only.
